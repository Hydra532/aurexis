Aurexis Client Privacy Policy

Aurexis Client may collect information users voluntarily provide when using our services or applying to our Creator Program.

When a user connects TikTok, Aurexis may access information authorized by the user, including TikTok profile information, account statistics, and video information.

This information is used to operate the Creator Program, review applications, verify submitted content, and provide related services.

Aurexis does not sell TikTok user data.

Users may request deletion of their stored information by contacting Aurexis support.
